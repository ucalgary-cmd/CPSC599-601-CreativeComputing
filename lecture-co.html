Dynamic Conversation

  • Mode of communication:person-to-person, face-to-face, realtime, improvised

Dynamic Creative Play

  • Mode of communication:casual, playful, realtime, improvised, shared experience
  • Today:passive, non-creative, isolating, anti-social
  • Vision:everyone participates/sees/understands the same thing

Mode of Communication
Presenting

Dynamic Presentation

  • Mode of communication:person-to-group, realtime, prepared outline but improvised details
  • Today:flexible blackboard or scripted static slides
  • Vision:sketch from scratch evidence-backed dynamic representations

Dynamic Stage

  • Mode of communication:spatial environment in which a presentation takes place
  • Today:presentation material without stage design vs play and set
  • Vision:mapping between concept/physical spaces, human-scale representations, layout is outline

Mode of Communication
Reading

Dynamic Reading

  • Mode of communication:media-to-person, solitary, deliberate, prolonged, contemplative
  • Today:concepts represented with words, mass-produced, one-size-fits-all
  • Vision:multi-channel, skimmable, transformable, explorable, context-sensitive, interrogable

Dynamic Spatial Media

  • Mode of communication:media-to-person, personal,prolonged, contemplative — but human-scale, and experienced with the body
  • Today:rectangular representations, focal vision, flat, intangible, hands neglected
  • Vision:Spatial textbooks, published and downloaded

Mode of Communication
Browsing, discovering, connecting

Dynamic Library

  • Mode of communication:walkable environment for browsing and discovering knowledge
  • Today:layouts of shelves of spines and books of papers conveying poor information
  • Vision:dynamic, multi-channel, at all time/distance scales, with spatially-anchored overview and connections

Research Gallery

  • Today:prior work displayed in space, less to learn than website/papers
  • Vision:intellectual environnment, physical presence, immersed, connections, space is a form of publication

Representation Gallery

  • Today:design of visualization and programming languages, but not of representations
  • Vision:dynamic representations, for generalization/instantiation/analogy on corpus of ideas

Mode of Communication
Writing

Dynamic Authoring

  • Mode of communication:person-to-media, deliberate, prolonged
  • Today:materials are static/manipulated or dynamic/scripted
  • Vision:directly manipulate representations of behavior and data

Mode of Communication
Creating new knowledge

Dynamic Mathematics

  • Mode of communication:set of tools for modeling physical systems, in order to understand, predict, and design them
  • Today:opaque representations, blind manipulation
  • Vision:evocative multimodal representations

Dynamic Objects To Think With

  • Mode of communication:representation that’s designed to be inspected and manipulated with the hands
  • Today:flat and intangible, ink on paper or pixels on a screen
  • Vision:dynamic abstract/symbolic concepts felt and manipulated by the fingers

Dynamic Environments To Think With

  • Mode of communication:representation that’s designed to be explored and manipulated with the body
  • Today:static design environments, paper or screens, immobile
  • Vision:human-scale spatial representations, physical tangible tools

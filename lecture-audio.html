Combining sound with other modalities

“Gestural control of sound synthesis”

Digital Musical Instruments

Computers enabled musical instrument players and creators to separate the control from the sound synthesis, that were traditionally bound together in acoustic instruments.

Cadoz gestes

  • Selection
  • Excitation
  • Modification

Methods to control sound

  • MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) (1982): serial communication over 8 bits of events aka control changes
  • OSC (Open Sound Control) (2002): URI-style messages over network
  • MPE (MIDI Polyphonic Extension) (2017): MIDI extension supporting parallel modification per note / stream (each in one channel)
  • MIDI 2.0 (2020): MIDI extension with: Property exchange, MIDI Capability Inquiry, Universal MIDI Packet, New protocol (precision and resolution)

Studio

Hack an interactive audio artifact

Find your interactive audio artifact

  • (Either) Pick a PlugData/PureData patch
    • (Easier - should work directly): PlugData’s Documentation Browser > Documentation > 3.audio.examples
    • (Harder - more complex and you might have to fix it): Store — plugdata
  • (Or) Find a sample that fits your individual pitch idea(s)
    • (Even Harder - even more complex and you might have to fix it): find an interactive audio example on GitHub (that fits your individual pitch)!

Install PlugData

plugdata — A visual programming environment for audio

PlugData Tips

  • First adjust your computer volume to halfway.
  • Then activate the DSP in PlugData (power icon on bottom-right).
  • Open documentation browser (square i icon on right side).
  • Start with testtone.pd.

Explore example patches

  • Documentation Browser > Documentation > 3.audio.examples
  • Store — plugdata

Modify your chosen artifact creatively

  • Focus on changes that will affect the sonic experience.
  • Make the sound more complex.
  • Combine examples if you wish!

Prepare your interactive presentation

  • Clarify the provenance (what example(s) you used).
  • Explain your intent, design idea.
  • Describe your creative updates.
  • Overview what you might have done next with more time.
